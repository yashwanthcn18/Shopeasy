Refund Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Returns

We accept returns within 7 days of delivery if the item is unused and in its original packaging.

How to return

  1. Email us at support@shopeasy.in with your order number.
  2. We will confirm and share return shipping instructions.
  3. Once we receive the item and verify its condition, we will process your refund.

Refund timeline

Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days to your original payment method.

Non-returnable items

Items that are damaged due to misuse or missing original packaging will not be accepted.
